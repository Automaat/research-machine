| **Sourcegraph** | Remote-first | ~$300-400k | ⚠️ Borderline |
| **Databricks** | EU offices (Amsterdam, etc.) | ~€250-400k | ⚠️ Possible |

### Hard Truth for EU-Remote

- **Most EU-remote roles pay 60-80% of US equivalent** — reaching $400k is rare
- **$400k+ in Europe** typically requires:
  - US company with location-agnostic pay (rare, shrinking)
  - London-based finance/quant (Jane Street, Citadel London)
  - Contracting/consulting at premium rates ($200+/hr)
  - Founding/early employee at well-funded startup with equity
- **Realistic EU Staff TC range:** €160k-300k at Tier 2/3 companies
- **To hit $400k+ from Poland:** Near-impossible with employment, possible with US contractor arrangement or location-agnostic company

### Most Promising EU-Remote Paths to $400k+

1. **GitLab Staff+** — Remote-first, transparent comp, location factors may still push close
2. **Stripe EU offices** — Dublin/London, competitive with US
3. **AI startups (Anthropic, Mistral)** — Equity upside could push TC over $400k
4. **Quant firms London** — Jane Street, Citadel, Two Sigma London offices
5. **US contractor arrangement** — $200-250/hr rate = $400-500k/yr, but no benefits/equity

## 🎯 Strategies to Reach $400k+ TC

### Strategy 1: Target Tier 1 Companies (Highest probability, requires US presence)

**Path:** Interview at 3-5 FAANG/Tier 1 simultaneously
**Timeline:** 3-6 months prep + interview cycle
**Requirements:**

- System design mastery (Designing Data-Intensive Applications level)
- LeetCode medium/hard proficiency
- Staff-level behavioral stories (cross-team impact, technical strategy)
- **Willing to relocate or work US hours** (dealbreaker for most Tier 1)

### Strategy 2: Multiple Competing Offers (Best negotiation leverage)

**Path:** Get 2-3 offers simultaneously, negotiate up
**Key tactics:**

- Equity is most negotiable component — push for more RSUs
- Mention competing offers by name (if well-known company)
- Target "top of band" — most companies have wide bands at Staff level
- Use services like [Levels.fyi Negotiation](https://www.levels.fyi/services/) or [Rora](https://www.teamrora.com/post/software-engineer-salary-negotiation)
- A level bump provides more long-term value than salary negotiation

### Strategy 3: AI/ML Pivot (Maximize premium from existing skills)

**Path:** Reposition as AI Infrastructure Engineer
**Your advantage:** Go + K8s + distributed systems = perfect AI infra foundation
**Concrete steps:**

1. Build projects with Ray, vLLM, or distributed training on K8s
2. Contribute to open-source AI infra (Ray, KubeFlow, vLLM)
3. Target: Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Anyscale, Modal, Together AI
4. Position: "I build the systems that make AI work at scale"

### Strategy 4: Quant/Finance Lateral (Highest TC ceiling)

**Path:** Apply to trading firms' London offices
**Your advantage:** Distributed systems, performance engineering
**Target:** Jane Street London, Citadel London, Two Sigma London, Jump Trading London
**Prep needed:** Heavy algo/data structures, systems programming, low-latency patterns
**Realistic TC:** $400k-800k+ in London

### Strategy 5: Equity Play (Long-term $400k+ through startup appreciation)

**Path:** Join well-funded AI startup with significant equity
**Target companies from your list:** Mistral, Modal, Anthropic, Databricks
**Math example:**

- Base: €180k + Stock: 0.05% of $5B company = $2.5M/4yr = $625k/yr pre-tax
- Total: ~$800k+ if company succeeds
- **Risk:** Equity can be worth $0

### Strategy 6: US Contractor/Consultant Path

**Path:** Contract at $200-250/hr for US companies
**Annual:** $400k-500k at 2000 hrs/yr
**Platforms:** Toptal (senior), A.Team, Gun.io
**Trade-offs:** No benefits, no equity, no stability, self-employment taxes
**Your advantage:** KubeCon speaker + Go + K8s is premium consultant profile
