## 1. Visa Options Overview

### H-1B: Specialty Occupation

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cap** | 85,000/year (65k general + 20k US master's) |
| **2026 Selection Rate** | ~21.8% (random lottery for FY2025-26) |
| **NEW: Weighted Lottery (March 2026+)** | Level IV wages: ~61% selection probability; Level III: ~38%; Level II: ~23%; Level I: ~15% |
| **Staff Engineer Impact** | $400k+ TC = Level IV wages = **~61% odds** under new system |
| **Timeline** | Registration Mar -> Lottery Apr -> File Apr-Jun -> Start Oct 1 |
| **Costs (from abroad)** | **$100,000 supplemental fee** (new) + $215 registration + $1,055 filing + $600 asylum fee |
| **Costs (if already in US)** | $215 + $1,055 + $600 (no $100k fee for change of status) |
| **Duration** | 3 years, extendable to 6 years |
| **Key Risk** | Lottery is annual gamble; $100k fee from abroad is brutal |

**Cap-Exempt Employers** (no lottery needed):

- Universities: Stanford, MIT, CMU, UC Berkeley, Michigan, Johns Hopkins
- Research institutions: NIH, NASA, DOE labs, national labs
- Nonprofit research orgs (expanded definition since Jan 2025)
- Can file year-round, no cap limits

### O-1A: Extraordinary Ability in Science/Business (NOT O-1B)

> **Important:** Software engineers qualify under **O-1A** (science/business), NOT O-1B (arts). O-1B is for arts/entertainment.

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cap** | **None** - file anytime |
| **Approval Rate** | ~93-94% (FY2025) |
| **Timeline** | 2-3 months prep + 15 business days (premium) = **~3 months total** |
| **Cost** | $8,000-$15,000 total (attorney + filing fees) |
| **Filing Fees** | $1,055 I-129 + $600 asylum fee + $2,805 premium processing |
| **Duration** | 3 years, renewable indefinitely |
| **Can Start Working** | Only after approval; with premium processing = ~15 business days after filing |

### L-1B: Intracompany Transfer (Specialized Knowledge)

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Path** | Join EU office -> work 12 months -> transfer to US |
| **Timeline** | 12 months EU + 2-4 months processing = **~14-16 months** |

### E-2: Treaty Investor

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Eligible?** | **Yes** - Poland has E-2 treaty with US |
| **Investment** | Typically >$150k (no legal minimum) |
| **Requirement** | 50%+ ownership of US business |
| **Duration** | 12 months (Poland-specific), renewable |
| **Relevance** | Low for employment path; requires owning/running a business |

### EB-1A: Extraordinary Ability (Green Card)

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Permanent residence (green card) directly |
| **Criteria** | Meet 3 of 10 criteria (similar to O-1A but higher bar) |
| **Processing** | 19 months standard; 45 days premium |
| **Cost** | $715 I-140 + $2,805 premium + attorney fees |
| **Poland Wait** | No backlog (unlike India/China) = **current** |
| **Strategy** | File concurrently with O-1A for dual track |

### EB-2 NIW: National Interest Waiver (Green Card)

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Permanent residence without employer sponsorship |
| **Approval Rate** | ~61% FY2025 (tightened for generic tech roles) |
| **Requirements** | Master's OR Bachelor's + 5yr progressive exp |
| **Key Test** | Specific endeavor with "national importance" |
| **Your Angle** | Cloud-native infrastructure, CNCF ecosystem, open source security |
| **Timeline** | 19 months standard; 45 days premium |
| **Risk** | 61% approval rate; USCIS tightened scrutiny for SW engineers |

## 2. O-1A Deep Dive (Best Path for Your Profile)

### Why O-1A is the Move

- **No cap/lottery** = predictable timeline
- **93-94% approval rate** vs H-1B's 21-61% lottery
- **~3 months** from start to working vs H-1B's 6-12 months
- **$8-15k cost** vs H-1B's potential $100k+ fee
- **No annual gambling** - file when ready
- Profile is strong: CNCF maintainer + KubeCon speaker + 10yr exp

### The 8 Criteria (Need 3 of 8)

| # | Criterion | Your Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Awards/prizes** for excellence | CNCF recognition, community awards | Medium |
| 2 | **Membership** in associations requiring outstanding achievement | CNCF maintainer status (selective), Linux Foundation | **Strong** |
| 3 | **Published material** about you in major media | Conference coverage, blog features, interviews | Medium |
| 4 | **Judging** others' work | KubeCon program committee, PR reviews on CNCF projects, CFP reviewer | **Strong** |
| 5 | **Original contributions** of major significance | CNCF core project contributions, adopted by major companies | **Strong** |
| 6 | **Scholarly articles** in professional journals | Conference papers, technical blog posts, CNCF documentation | **Strong** |
| 7 | **Employment in critical/essential capacity** for distinguished organizations | Staff Engineer at [current employer] | Medium-Strong |
| 8 | **High salary/remuneration** | $400k+ offer letter (top 5-10% of field) | **Strong** |

### Evidence Mapping for Your Profile

**Criterion 2 - Membership (CNCF Maintainer):**

- CNCF project maintainership is highly selective
- Document: nomination process, approval criteria, percentage of contributors who become maintainers
- Get letters from other CNCF leaders confirming selectivity
- This is one of the strongest pieces - open source foundations have strict governance

**Criterion 4 - Judging (KubeCon + PR Reviews):**

- KubeCon CFP program committee membership
- Reviewing/approving PRs on CNCF projects with rigorous standards
- Conference talk selection committee participation
- Document: invitation emails, reviewer dashboards, acceptance rates
- Projects like Kubernetes accept <small% of contributions - document this

**Criterion 5 - Original Contributions:**

- CNCF project features you designed/built
- GitHub stars, adoption metrics, production usage at major companies
- Projects with 5,000+ stars strongly satisfy this criterion
- Document: download/usage stats, company adoption, industry impact

**Criterion 6 - Scholarly Articles:**

- KubeCon presentations (nationally/internationally recognized conference)
- Conference proceedings, published talks
- Technical blog posts with significant readership
- Document: acceptance rate, audience size, citation count

**Criterion 8 - High Salary:**

- $400k+ offer letter from US company
- Compare to OES/BLS wage data showing top 5-10% for occupation
- This is straightforward with the right offer

### O-1A Success Factors

- **Approval rate:** 93-94% overall; likely higher for strong STEM profiles
- **Advisory opinion:** Get from IEEE, ACM, or relevant peer group (recommended but not required)
- **Letters of recommendation:** 5-8 from industry experts who can speak to extraordinary ability
- **Attorney selection:** Critical - use immigration firm specializing in O-1A for tech (Fragomen, Berry Appleman, Manifest Law, BeyondBorder)

### O-1A Timeline

```
Month 1-2:  Evidence gathering, attorney engagement, petition drafting
Month 2-3:  Advisory opinion (if seeking), recommendation letters
Month 3:    File I-129 with premium processing ($2,805)
Month 3+15d: USCIS decision (approve/RFE/deny)
Month 4:    Consular processing at US Embassy Warsaw (~2 weeks)
Month 4-5:  **Start working in US**
```

### Can You Work While Processing?

- **No** - cannot work until O-1A is approved and visa stamped
- With premium processing: decision in 15 business days
- If RFE (Request for Evidence): adds 2-4 weeks
- **Practical timeline from filing to working: 4-8 weeks with premium**

## 6. Recommended Strategy

### Priority Path: O-1A (Fastest, Most Reliable)

```
NOW:        Start evidence gathering, engage O-1A attorney
Month 1:    Apply to target companies (Anthropic, Google, Databricks)
Month 1-2:  Collect recommendation letters (5-8)
Month 2:    Get offer letter ($400k+ = high salary evidence)
Month 2-3:  File O-1A with premium processing
Month 3:    Approval (~15 business days)
Month 3-4:  Consular stamp at US Embassy Warsaw
Month 4:    Start working in US
```

### Parallel Track: EB-1A Green Card

```
Month 3-4:  After O-1A approval, file EB-1A I-140 (premium)
Month 4-5:  EB-1A approval (45 days premium)
Month 5-6:  File I-485 adjustment of status (or consular processing)
            Poland = no backlog = current priority date
Month 12-18: Green card in hand
```

### Backup: H-1B (Only if O-1A Fails)

- Under new weighted lottery (March 2026+): ~61% odds at Level IV wages
- $100k fee from abroad makes this expensive
- Consider only as backup

### Nuclear Option: L-1B through EU Office

- Join Google/Microsoft Warsaw office
- Transfer after 12 months
- Slowest but most guaranteed path

## Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| O-1A denial | ~6% | Strong profile; RFE response adds 2-4 weeks |
| H-1B lottery loss | 39% (Level IV) | O-1A as primary path |
