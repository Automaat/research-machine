## 3. Visa Situation - Polish Citizen in UK Post-Brexit

### Do You Need a Visa?

- **Yes.** Since Brexit (Jan 1, 2021), Polish citizens need a work visa to work in the UK
- EU Settlement Scheme deadline passed (June 30, 2021) for those already living in UK
- For new arrivals: Skilled Worker visa is the primary route

### Skilled Worker Visa Requirements (2025/2026)

- **Minimum salary:** £41,700/year (raised July 2025) - easily met at target roles
- **Skill level:** RQF Level 6 (graduate level) required since July 2025 - software engineering qualifies
- **English language:** B2 level required since January 8, 2026 (higher than previous A1/B1)
- **Employer sponsorship:** Must have Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) from licensed employer
- **Visa fee:** £922-£1,821
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** £1,035/year
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years; can lead to settlement (ILR) after 5 years

### How Easy Is Sponsorship?

- **Very easy** at these companies - all are sophisticated employers with dedicated immigration teams
- At $400K+ salary levels, meeting the £41,700 threshold is trivial
- Process typically takes 2-8 weeks for CoS issuance
- B2 English requirement is straightforward for any professional
- **Key advantage:** All major quant firms and FAANG regularly sponsor Skilled Worker visas

### ETA Requirement (for visits)

- Since Feb 25, 2026: Polish citizens must have Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) even for visits
- Cost: £10/€19, valid 2 years
- Strictly enforced - cannot board transport to UK without approved ETA

## 4. Tax Analysis - What Does $400K Net in London?

### Scenario: £320,000 Annual Salary (~$400K at ~1.25 USD/GBP)

Using [UK Tax Calculator](https://uk.talent.com/tax-calculator/Great+Britain-400000) for £400K:

| Component | Annual | Monthly |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Gross Salary** | £400,000 | £33,333 |
| Income Tax | -£159,299 | -£13,275 |
| National Insurance | -£16,361 | -£1,363 |
| **Total Tax** | **-£175,661** | **-£14,638** |
| **Net Pay** | **£224,339** | **£18,695** |

- **Effective tax rate:** 43.9%
- **Marginal tax rate:** 48.3% (45% income tax + 2% NI + 1.25% dividend)
- **No personal allowance** at this income (removed above £125,140)
- Employer also pays £60,200 in NI contributions

### Realistic Scenarios by TC Level

| TC (GBP) | TC (USD ~1.25) | Net (GBP) | Net (USD) | Effective Rate |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| £250,000 | $312K | ~£157K | ~$196K | ~37% |
| £320,000 | $400K | ~£196K | ~$245K | ~39% |
| £400,000 | $500K | ~£224K | ~$280K | ~44% |
| £500,000 | $625K | ~£269K | ~$336K | ~46% |

**Note:** Stock/RSU compensation is taxed as income in UK. Bonuses taxed at marginal rate.
