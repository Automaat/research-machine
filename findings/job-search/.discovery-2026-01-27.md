## Key Insights

### What's Hot in 2025-2026

- **Kubernetes platforms** continue strong funding (CAST AI $108M, Spectro $75M)
- **AI infrastructure** massive rounds (Together AI $305M, Nscale $1.5B)
- **Developer platforms** growing (Port $100M, GitLab/Sourcegraph scale-ups)
- **European AI** catching up (Mistral, Nscale, Multiverse Computing)

### Compensation Trends

- Series B-C companies: €120-180k range for senior eng
- Eastern EU (Lithuania, Poland): €78-116k (below target)
- Western EU (UK, Germany, France): €140-180k (target range)
- Equity becoming significant (early Series C can 2-3x comp)

### Remote EU Reality Check

- "Remote-first" != "Hire anywhere in EU" (check carefully)
- US companies: Often US/Canada only or very limited EU
- EU companies: More likely true EU remote
- UK post-Brexit: Sometimes treated separately

### Tech Stack Patterns

- Golang dominates cloud-native/K8s platforms
- Python heavy in AI/ML research companies
- TypeScript/JS for developer tools/frontend
- Distributed systems = Go almost always
