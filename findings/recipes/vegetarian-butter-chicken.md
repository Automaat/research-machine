# 🍛 Vegetarian Butter Chicken (Tofu Makhani)

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 35m | **Cook:** 35m | **Total:** 70m
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Medium
**Dietary:** 🥬 Vegetarian | 🌱 Vegan (with vegan butter)

## 🥗 Ingredients

### Tofu

- 450g (16 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed
- 30ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 15g (1 tbsp) cornstarch
- 3g (½ tsp) salt

### Sauce

- 30g (2 tbsp) vegan butter or ghee
- 160g (1 large) onion, diced small
- 15g (1 tbsp) fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 5g (1 tsp) curry powder
- 5g (1 tsp) ground coriander
- 1-2g (¼ tsp) cayenne pepper
- 5g (1 tsp) salt
- 90g (3 oz / 6 tbsp) tomato paste
- 400ml (13.5 oz can) full-fat coconut milk
- 15g (1 tbsp) garam masala

### Serving

- 740g (4 cups) cooked basmati rice
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Naan bread (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Quick Reference)

1. Press tofu 20-30 minutes; preheat oven to 200°C (400°F)
2. Tear tofu into bite-sized pieces; coat with oil, cornstarch, salt
3. Bake 20-25 minutes until golden, checking at 20 minutes
4. Sauté onion in butter 3-4 minutes
5. Add ginger, garlic; cook 1 minute
6. Add curry powder, coriander, cayenne, tomato paste; cook 2 minutes
7. Add coconut milk; simmer 5-10 minutes
8. Stir in garam masala; simmer 2-3 minutes more
9. Add baked tofu; coat well; serve over rice with cilantro

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Detailed)

### Mise en Place (Required)

1. **Prep ingredients** — Press tofu between paper towels or in tofu press for 20-30 minutes. While pressing, measure all ingredients. Dice onion small, grate ginger, mince garlic. Have all spices measured and ready (Indian cooking moves quickly once started).

2. **Prepare equipment** — Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line baking sheet with parchment paper. Set up station with ziplock bag for tofu coating, large pan for sauce.

### Cook Tofu

3. **Tear tofu for texture** — Instead of cutting into uniform cubes, tear pressed tofu into irregular bite-sized pieces (2-3cm). Irregular shapes create more surface area for crisping and better sauce adhesion.
   > 💡 *Why:* Tearing creates craggy surfaces that crisp beautifully and trap sauce in crevices—much better than smooth cubed surfaces.

4. **Coat and bake** — Add torn tofu, olive oil, cornstarch, and salt to ziplock bag. Seal and shake gently to coat evenly. Spread on prepared baking sheet in single layer. Bake 20-25 minutes, checking at 20 minutes, until golden and crispy at edges.
   > ⚠️ *Note:* Torn pieces crisp faster than cubes—start checking at 20 minutes to avoid over-drying.

### Build Sauce

5. **Sauté aromatics** — Melt vegan butter in large pan over medium-high heat. Add diced onion and cook 3-4 minutes until softened and lightly golden, stirring occasionally.

6. **Add ginger and garlic** — Add grated ginger and minced garlic. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly.
   > 💡 *Why:* Garlic added after onions prevents burning (garlic burns faster due to higher sugar content).

7. **Bloom base spices** — Add curry powder, ground coriander, cayenne pepper, and tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently, until paste darkens and becomes fragrant.
   > 💡 *Why:* Blooming spices in fat activates flavor compounds; cooking tomato paste removes raw taste and concentrates sweetness.

8. **Simmer sauce** — Pour in coconut milk and stir until smooth. Bring to simmer, reduce heat to medium-low, and cook 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.

9. **Add garam masala** — Stir in garam masala and simmer 2-3 minutes more.
   > 💡 *Why:* Garam masala added near end preserves delicate aromatic compounds that dissipate with prolonged cooking.

### Finish

10. **Combine and serve** — Add baked tofu pieces to sauce. Gently stir to coat thoroughly. Taste and adjust salt if needed. Serve immediately over basmati rice, garnished with fresh cilantro. Naan on the side optional.

## 🔄 Substitutions

| Ingredient | Substitute | Notes |
|------------|------------|-------|
| Extra-firm tofu | Paneer (450g) | For vegetarian not vegan; skip pressing, cube and pan-fry until golden |
| Vegan butter | Ghee | Traditional option for non-vegan |
| Coconut milk | Cashew cream (120g cashews blended with 280ml water) | Less coconut flavor, equally creamy |
| Fresh ginger | 5g (1 tsp) ground ginger | Less vibrant but works in pinch |
| Garam masala | DIY blend: 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp coriander + ½ tsp cardamom + ¼ tsp cinnamon | Approximate flavor profile |

## 📝 Tips & Variations

- **Make ahead:** Sauce can be made 2 days ahead; refrigerate separately from tofu. Reheat gently and add tofu just before serving.
- **Freezer friendly:** Freeze sauce (without tofu) up to 3 months. Tofu best fresh; freezing changes texture (chewier).
- **Spice level:** Adjust cayenne 0-½ tsp for mild to hot. Add fresh jalapeño with aromatics for more heat.
- **Creamier sauce:** Add 60ml (¼ cup) more coconut milk or 60g (¼ cup) cashew cream at end.
- **Protein swap:** Use chickpeas (2 cans/800g drained) instead of tofu—skip baking, add directly to sauce.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate together (tofu in sauce absorbs flavor) 3-4 days in airtight container.
