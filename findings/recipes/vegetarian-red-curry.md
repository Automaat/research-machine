# 🍛 Vegetarian Thai Red Curry

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 15m | **Cook:** 30m | **Total:** 45m
**Difficulty:** ⭐ Easy
**Dietary:** 🥬 Vegetarian | 🌱 Vegan | 🌾 Gluten-Free (check paste) | 🥛 Dairy-Free

## 🥗 Ingredients

### Main

- 30ml (2 tbsp) coconut oil, divided
- 227g (8 oz) super firm tofu, cut into 1.25cm (½-inch) cubes
- 1 medium onion, sliced into strips
- 60ml (¼ cup) vegan red curry paste (plus more to taste)
- 400ml (14 oz can) light coconut milk
- 240ml (1 cup) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 medium russet potato, scrubbed and cut into 1.25cm (½-inch) pieces
- 200g (2 cups) broccoli florets (about 1 small crown)
- 1 medium red bell pepper, roughly diced
- 1 medium carrot, sliced
- 30ml (2 tbsp) fresh lime juice
- 10g (2 tsp) organic brown sugar
- 60ml (¼ cup) fresh basil leaves, chopped
- Salt to taste

### For Serving

- Cooked jasmine rice (about 750g / 4 cups cooked)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Quick Reference)

1. Pat tofu dry; pan-fry in oil until golden/crispy (~10 min)
2. Sauté onion until softened with light browning (~7-10 min)
3. Bloom curry paste with onions (~2 min)
4. Add coconut milks + potato; boil then simmer covered (~10-12 min)
5. Add broccoli, pepper, carrot; simmer uncovered (~5 min)
6. Stir in tofu; simmer 1 min
7. Off heat: add lime juice, sugar, basil, salt; serve over rice

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Detailed)

### Mise en Place (5 minutes)

1. **Prep ingredients** — Pat tofu cubes dry with paper towel (critical for browning). Slice onion into strips. Cut potato into 1.25cm cubes (uniform size ensures even cooking). Cut broccoli into florets, dice bell pepper, slice carrot. Chop basil. Juice lime.
   > 💡 *Why:* Curry comes together quickly once cooking starts; having everything prepped prevents overcooking while you scramble for ingredients.

### Cook Tofu (10 minutes)

2. **Pan-fry tofu** — Heat 15ml (1 tbsp) coconut oil in nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add tofu cubes in single layer. Cook approximately 10 minutes, flipping occasionally with spatula, until golden and crispy on multiple sides. Transfer to plate.
   > 💡 *Why:* Pre-cooking tofu separately develops texture and prevents it from soaking up too much curry liquid. Super firm tofu holds shape best.

### Build Curry Base (20 minutes)

3. **Sauté aromatics** — Melt remaining 15ml coconut oil in large pot over medium heat. Add onion strips and cook 7-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until softened and beginning to show light brown edges.
   > ⚠️ *Note:* Don't rush this step. Softened onions add sweetness; light browning adds depth.

4. **Bloom curry paste** — Stir in curry paste with the onion for about 2 minutes, stirring constantly to prevent burning. Paste should become fragrant and slightly darken.
   > 💡 *Why:* "Blooming" spices in fat releases fat-soluble flavor compounds. This is the secret to restaurant-quality curry depth.

5. **Simmer with potato** — Add both coconut milks and potato pieces. Increase heat to high and bring to a boil. Lower heat to maintain gentle simmer, cover, and cook approximately 10-12 minutes until potato pieces can be pierced with fork but still have slight resistance. Stir occasionally and scrape pot bottom to prevent sticking.
   > 💡 *Why:* Potato takes longest to cook, so it goes in first. Simmering (not boiling) prevents coconut milk from breaking.

6. **Add quick-cook vegetables** — Uncover and add broccoli florets, bell pepper, and carrot. Continue simmering uncovered for about 5 minutes until vegetables are tender but still have slight bite.
   > 💡 *Why:* Uncovered simmer reduces sauce slightly for better consistency. Vegetables added by cooking time ensures everything finishes together.

### Finish (2 minutes)

7. **Add tofu** — Stir in the cooked tofu cubes and simmer 1 minute longer to heat through.

8. **Balance flavors** — Remove from heat. Add lime juice, brown sugar, and basil. Stir to combine. Season with salt to taste (start with ½ tsp, add more if needed).
   > 💡 *Why:* Acid (lime) and sugar added off heat preserves brightness. Basil at end keeps it vibrant green. Salt enhances all other flavors.

9. **Serve** — Ladle curry over cooked jasmine rice in bowls.

## 🔄 Substitutions

| Ingredient | Substitute | Notes |
|------------|------------|-------|
| Super firm tofu | Pressed extra-firm tofu | Press 30 min wrapped in towels; won't be quite as crispy |
| Super firm tofu | Chickpeas (400g can, drained) | For tofu-free version; add with vegetables in step 6 |
| Russet potato | Sweet potato | Similar cook time; adds natural sweetness |
| Broccoli | Cauliflower, green beans, snap peas | Match size for similar cook times |
| Full-fat coconut milk | All light coconut milk | Less rich but lower calorie; curry will be thinner |
| Red curry paste | Green curry paste | Different flavor profile but same technique |
| Basil | Cilantro or Thai basil | Thai basil more authentic if available |
| Lime juice | Lemon juice | Less traditional but provides needed acidity |

## 📝 Tips & Variations

- **Spice level:** Start with 60ml curry paste; add more at step 8 if desired. Curry paste brands vary wildly in heat.
- **Make ahead:** Curry tastes even better next day as flavors meld. Refrigerate up to 4 days. Add basil when reheating.
- **Freezer friendly:** Freeze without basil/lime for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight, reheat, add fresh basil/lime.
- **Protein variations:** Use crispy chickpeas, edamame, or tempeh instead of tofu
- **Vegetable additions:** Try bamboo shoots, baby corn, mushrooms (especially oyster or shiitake), eggplant, zucchini
- **Umami boost:** Add 1 tbsp soy sauce or tamari with the lime juice for deeper flavor (traditionally Thai cooking uses fish sauce, but soy works for vegetarian)
- **Coconut cream trick:** Spoon thick coconut cream from top of chilled can, fry curry paste in it until oil separates (~3-4 min), then add remaining coconut liquid. This is traditional technique for maximum flavor.
- **Thai accompaniments:** Serve with lime wedges, sliced Thai chilies, extra basil, and cucumber slices on the side
