# 🍲 Fasolka po Bretońsku (Vegetarian Polish Beans)

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 15m + overnight soak | **Cook:** 2h | **Total:** ~2h15m + overnight
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Medium
**Dietary:** 🥬 Vegetarian | 🌱 Vegan | 🌾 Gluten-Free | 🥛 Dairy-Free

## 🥗 Ingredients

### Main

- 500g (2½ cups) dried white beans (preferably "Piękny Jaś" variety)
- 600g (2½ cups) tomato passata
- 60g (4 tbsp) tomato concentrate
- 1 large onion (160g), diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 30ml (2 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 180ml (¾ cup) water
- 15ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce

### Spices

- 3 bay leaves
- 6 allspice berries
- 3 black peppercorns
- 15g (1 tbsp) dried marjoram
- 5g (1 tsp) smoked sweet paprika (not hot)
- 2.5g (½ tsp) ground caraway
- 1.25g (¼ tsp) ground coriander
- 1.25g (¼ tsp) dried chili
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Optional Enrichments

- 200g sliced mushrooms (for depth)
- 2.5g (½ tsp) lovage
- 2.5g (½ tsp) dried parsley

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Quick Reference)

1. Soak beans overnight (8-12 hours) in water covering by 10cm
2. Drain, rinse, cook beans 90 min in 1L fresh water until tender; do NOT salt
3. Sauté onion in oil until translucent (8 min)
4. Add garlic, bay leaves, allspice, peppercorns; cook 5 min
5. Add passata, concentrate, water, soy sauce, all spices; simmer 6 min
6. Add drained beans; simmer covered 20 min on low
7. Check consistency—add reserved bean water if dry
8. Adjust salt/pepper; rest 10 min before serving

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Detailed)

### Day Before: Soak Beans

1. **Soak beans overnight** — Rinse 500g dried white beans. Place in large bowl and cover with water at least 10cm above bean level. Soak 8-12 hours at room temperature. Do NOT soak longer than 12 hours.
   > 💡 *Why:* Soaking reduces cooking time, improves digestibility, and helps beans cook evenly

### Day Of: Mise en Place (Required)

2. **Prep ingredients** — Drain and thoroughly rinse soaked beans. Dice onion. Mince garlic. Measure all spices. Have tomato passata and concentrate ready.
   > ⚠️ *Note:* Don't skip rinsing after soaking—removes starches that cause foam

3. **Prepare equipment** — Set out large pot for beans, large heavy-bottomed pot for sauce, strainer, measuring cup for bean water

### Cook Beans

4. **Cook beans** — Place drained beans in large pot with 1L fresh cold water. Bring to boil, reduce to gentle simmer. Cook uncovered ~90 minutes until tender (bean should crush easily between fingers). Do NOT add salt yet. Reserve 500ml bean cooking water before draining.
   > 💡 *Why:* Salt added during cooking toughens bean skins and dramatically extends cook time. Wait until beans are tender.

### Build Sauce Base

5. **Sauté aromatics** — Heat 30ml oil in large heavy pot over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and translucent (not browned).
   > 💡 *Why:* Gentle cooking develops sweetness without bitterness

6. **Bloom spices** — Add minced garlic, bay leaves, allspice berries, and peppercorns. Stir constantly for 5 minutes. Garlic should be fragrant but not browned.
   > ⚠️ *Note:* Garlic burns faster than onion—adding after prevents bitterness. Stirring constantly prevents scorching.

### Combine & Simmer

7. **Build tomato base** — Add 600g passata, 60g tomato concentrate, 180ml water, 15ml soy sauce, marjoram, smoked paprika, caraway, coriander, and chili. Stir thoroughly. Bring to gentle simmer and cook uncovered 6 minutes, stirring occasionally.
   > 💡 *Why:* Brief simmer melds spices and concentrates tomato flavor

8. **Add beans** — Add drained cooked beans to sauce. Stir gently to coat. Cover pot, reduce heat to low, and simmer 20 minutes. Stir every 5 minutes to prevent sticking.

### Finish

9. **Adjust consistency** — After 20 min, check sauce. Beans should be in rich, thick sauce—not dry. If too thick, add reserved bean cooking water 120ml (½ cup) at a time until desired consistency. If too thin, simmer uncovered 5 more minutes.
   > 💡 *Why:* Beans absorb liquid as they sit. Sauce should be abundant—dish thickens significantly when cooled.

10. **Season and rest** — Taste and adjust salt (start with 5g / 1 tsp) and black pepper. Remove from heat, cover, and rest 10 minutes before serving.
    > 💡 *Why:* Resting allows flavors to meld and temperature to equilibrate for safer eating

## 🔄 Substitutions

| Ingredient | Substitute | Notes |
|------------|------------|-------|
| Dried white beans | 4 cans (400g each) white beans | Drain, rinse; skip bean cooking steps 4, add in step 8 |
| Tomato passata | 3 cans (400g) crushed tomatoes | Slightly chunkier texture |
| Soy sauce | 1 tbsp liquid aminos OR ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp umami seasoning | For soy-free |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tsp sweet paprika + ¼ tsp liquid smoke | If smoked paprika unavailable |
| Dried marjoram | 3 tbsp fresh marjoram, added last 5 min | Fresh herbs more delicate |

## 📝 Tips & Variations

- **Make ahead:** Flavor improves **dramatically** if refrigerated overnight and reheated. This dish is genuinely better day 2 as starches redistribute.
- **Freezer friendly:** Freezes excellently for up to 3 months. Thaw in fridge overnight, reheat gently.
- **Canned bean shortcut:** Use 4 cans (400g each) drained white beans instead of dried. Skip soaking/cooking, add directly in step 8. Reduces active time to 45 minutes.
- **Mushroom depth:** Sauté 200g sliced mushrooms with onions in step 5 for meaty umami depth
- **Common mistake:** Using hot smoked paprika instead of sweet makes dish unexpectedly spicy—verify label
- **Bean selection:** Smaller beans cook faster. "Piękny Jaś" is traditional but any white bean works (cannellini, navy, great northern)
- **Bread pairing:** Serve with crusty bread to soak up sauce—traditional Polish serving style
