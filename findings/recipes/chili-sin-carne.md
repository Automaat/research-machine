# 🌶️ Chili Sin Carne

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 15m | **Cook:** 50m | **Total:** 65m
**Difficulty:** ⭐ Easy
**Dietary:** 🥬 Vegetarian | 🌱 Vegan | 🌾 Gluten-Free

## 🥗 Ingredients

### Aromatics & Base

- 30ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 160g (1 medium) onion, diced
- 128g (1 medium) carrot, diced
- 1 medium red bell pepper, diced (about 120g)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced

### Spices & Umami

- 15ml (1 tbsp) tomato paste
- 15g (1 tbsp) chili powder
- 7g (1 tbsp) ground cumin
- 5g (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 2g (½ tsp) dried oregano
- 2g (¼ tsp) cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
- 15ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce or tamari

### Liquids & Beans

- 800g (28 oz / 2 cans) crushed tomatoes
- 240ml (1 cup) vegetable broth
- 400g (14 oz / 1 can) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 400g (14 oz / 1 can) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 400g (14 oz / 1 can) pinto beans, drained and rinsed

### Finishing

- 5ml (1 tsp) sherry vinegar or fresh lime juice
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, diced avocado, sour cream/vegan sour cream (for serving)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

### Prep

1. **Mise en place** — Dice onion, carrot, and bell pepper into ~1cm pieces. Mince garlic. Drain and rinse all beans. Measure spices.

### Cook

2. **Sauté aromatics** — Heat olive oil in large heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, carrot, and bell pepper. Cook 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until onion is translucent and carrot is softened.
   > 💡 *Why:* Softening vegetables releases natural sugars and builds flavor foundation. Carrots need slightly longer than onions to soften fully.

3. **Add garlic and bloom spices** — Add minced garlic, cook 30-60 seconds until fragrant (don't let burn). Add tomato paste, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and cayenne (if using). Cook 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly to coat vegetables.
   > 💡 *Why:* Blooming spices in fat releases fat-soluble flavor compounds. Tomato paste caramelizes slightly, adding umami depth. Garlic goes in before spices to avoid burning during longer spice bloom.

4. **Add liquids and beans** — Pour in crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, and soy sauce. Stir well, scraping up any browned bits (fond) from bottom of pot. Add all three types of beans. Stir to combine.
   > 💡 *Why:* Fond contains concentrated flavors from Maillard reaction. Deglazing captures these flavors in the chili.

5. **Simmer uncovered** — Bring to boil, then reduce heat to maintain gentle simmer. Cook uncovered 30-45 minutes, stirring every 5-10 minutes, until chili has thickened and flavors have melded. Add water 60ml (¼ cup) at a time if chili becomes too thick.
   > 💡 *Why:* Uncovered simmering reduces liquid for thicker consistency. Long simmer allows flavors to integrate. Tomatoes become sweeter and less acidic with cooking time.

### Finish

6. **Add acid and season** — Remove from heat. Stir in sherry vinegar or lime juice. Taste and season with salt and black pepper.
   > 💡 *Why:* Acid added at end brightens flavors without cooking off. Beans and soy sauce add salt, so taste before adding more.

7. **Optional: thicken** — For thicker, creamier texture, transfer 240ml (1 cup) chili to blender, blend until smooth, return to pot and stir.
   > 💡 *Why:* Blended beans release starch, creating natural thickener without flour or cornstarch.

8. **Serve** — Ladle into bowls. Top with fresh cilantro, diced avocado, sour cream, shredded cheese, or tortilla chips as desired.

## 📝 Tips & Variations

- **Make ahead:** Chili tastes better next day after flavors meld. Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze up to 3 months.
- **Spice level:** Omit cayenne for mild. Add 1-2 diced jalapeños with aromatics for medium heat. Add chipotle peppers in adobo for smoky heat.
- **Protein boost:** Add 200g (7 oz) TVP (textured vegetable protein) or crumbled tempeh in step 4 for meatier texture.
- **Smoky variation:** Use fire-roasted canned tomatoes and increase smoked paprika to 10g (2 tsp).
- **Instant Pot:** Sauté in pot using "Sauté" mode (steps 2-4), then pressure cook on high 10 minutes with quick release.
- **Slow cooker:** Sauté aromatics and spices in pan first, transfer to slow cooker with remaining ingredients. Cook on low 6-8 hours or high 3-4 hours.
- **Thicker chili:** Mash some beans against pot side with wooden spoon during cooking.
- **Serving ideas:** Over rice, with cornbread, in tacos, over baked potatoes, or as hot dog topping.
