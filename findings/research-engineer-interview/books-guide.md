## 🤖 Machine Learning

### Foundational Theory

| Book | Author | Level | Best For |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| ⭐ **[Pattern Recognition & ML](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf)** | Christopher Bishop | Advanced | Bayesian ML, theory depth |
| **[Elements of Statistical Learning](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/)** | Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman | Advanced | Classical ML theory |
| **[Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/)** | James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani | Intermediate | Accessible version of ESL |
| **[Foundations of ML](https://cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/mlbook/)** | Mohri, Rostamizadeh, Talwalkar | Advanced | Learning theory, PAC |

**Why Bishop is essential:** "The bible of Bayesian machine learning" - covers everything from probability to graphical models with mathematical rigor. 400+ exercises. Used in top university courses worldwide.

### Practical Implementation

| Book | Author | Level | Best For |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| ⭐ **[Hands-On ML with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TF](https://www.amazon.com/Hands-Machine-Learning-Scikit-Learn-TensorFlow/dp/1098125975)** | Aurélien Géron | Beginner-Intermediate | Best balance of theory + code |
| **[Machine Learning Yearning](https://www.deeplearning.ai/programs/machine-learning-yearning/)** | Andrew Ng | All levels | Project structuring, decisions |
| **[The Hundred-Page ML Book](https://themlbook.com/)** | Andriy Burkov | Beginner | Quick comprehensive overview |

**Why Géron is essential:** "Hands down the most perfect balance between beautifully explained examples and workable code." Gradual difficulty progression, excellent illustrations.

## 🧠 Deep Learning

| Book | Author | Level | Best For |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| ⭐ **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** | Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville | Advanced | Comprehensive theory |
| **[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/)** | Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola | Intermediate | Interactive, code-first |
| **[Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/)** | Michael Nielsen | Beginner | Intuitive intro |

**Why Goodfellow is essential:** "The Bible of Deep Learning" - 150 pages of math background, 300 pages of core DL, 200 pages of research topics. Written by inventors of GANs. Free PDF available.

### Goodfellow vs Géron

| Aspect | Deep Learning (Goodfellow) | Hands-On ML (Géron) |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| **Approach** | Theoretical, academic | Practical, hands-on |
| **Code** | Minimal | Extensive (Python) |
| **Math** | Heavy, rigorous | Present but accessible |
| **Scope** | Deep learning only | ML + Deep Learning |
| **Best for** | Understanding why | Understanding how |

**💡 Recommendation:** Read in tandem - Géron for implementation, Goodfellow for theory

## 📊 Reading Order Recommendation

### Phase 1: Foundations (Month 1-2)
1. **[Mathematics for ML](https://mml-book.github.io/)** - Math foundations
2. **[The Hundred-Page ML Book](https://themlbook.com/)** - Quick ML overview

### Phase 2: Core ML (Month 2-3)
3. **[Hands-On ML](https://www.amazon.com/Hands-Machine-Learning-Scikit-Learn-TensorFlow/dp/1098125975)** - Practical implementation
4. **[Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/)** - Theory complement

### Phase 3: Deep Learning (Month 3-4)
5. **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** - Theory bible
6. **[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/)** - Interactive practice

### Phase 4: Specialization (Month 4-5)
7. **[NLP with Transformers](https://transformersbook.com/)** OR **[Sutton & Barto RL](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html)** - Domain depth
8. **[Pattern Recognition & ML](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf)** - Advanced theory

### Phase 5: Interview (Month 5-6)
9. **[ML Interviews Book](https://huyenchip.com/ml-interviews-book/)** - Interview prep
10. **[Designing ML Systems](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Machine-Learning-Systems-Production-Ready/dp/1098107969)** - System design
