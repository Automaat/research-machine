## 📆 Week 1-2: Paper Replication

### Why Replicate?

- Proves you can go from paper → code
- Deep understanding of methods
- Interview talking point
- Potential contribution to community

### Paper Selection Criteria

- Recent (< 2 years old)
- Feasible compute requirements
- Clear methodology
- Available dataset
- Not already 100% replicated

### Replication Process

1. **Read paper 3+ times**
   - Understand every equation
   - Note ambiguities

2. **Study reference code (if available)**
   - After attempting yourself
   - Note implementation details not in paper

3. **Implement step by step**
   - Start simple, add complexity
   - Unit test components

4. **Compare results**
   - Don't expect exact match
   - Document discrepancies

5. **Write up findings**
   - What was unclear?
   - What did you learn?
   - Where did your results differ?

## 💡 Tips for Paper Replication

1. **Expect discrepancies** - Papers often omit details
2. **Contact authors** - Most respond to clarifying questions
3. **Check issues** - Others may have same questions
4. **Document everything** - Future you will thank you
5. **Start small** - Replicate a subset first

---

## 💡 Tips for Open Source

1. **Start small** - Documentation, typos, small bugs
2. **Read existing PRs** - Understand the style
3. **Be patient** - Reviews take time
4. **Be responsive** - Address feedback quickly
5. **Build relationships** - Regular contributions matter more than big ones
