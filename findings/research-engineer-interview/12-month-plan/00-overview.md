## ⚡ 6-Month vs 12-Month Comparison

| Aspect | 6 Months | 12 Months |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Total hours | 500-600 | 800-1000 |
| Weekly commitment | 20-25 hrs (intensive) | 15-20 hrs (sustainable) |
| Depth | Interview-ready | Research-ready |
| Projects | 1-2 solid | 4-5 substantial |
| Papers read | 10-15 | 40-50 |
| Domains | 1 specialization | 2 deep, 1 broad |
| Algorithms implemented | 5-10 | 15-20 |
| Risk | Faster feedback | More investment before validation |

---

## 🎯 Key Advantages of 12-Month Plan

**Grounded in research:**

1. **More paper depth** - Target 40-50 papers (vs 10-15)
2. **More iteration cycles** - 2x the practice-learn-practice loops
3. **Dual specialization** - Go deep in 2 domains

**Additional benefits:**

1. **Build public presence** - Blog, Twitter, GitHub profile
2. **Real ML work mid-journey** - Internal transfer, part-time projects possible
3. **Paper replication** - Stronger research credibility
4. **Sustainable pace** - 15-20 hrs/week vs 20-25 hrs/week

---

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Losing momentum | Set weekly goals, accountability partner |
| Market changes | Stay current with arXiv, Twitter ML community |
| Over-preparation | Start applying at month 9-10 for feedback |
| Direction uncertainty | Informational interviews at month 6 |

### 💡 Mid-Journey Real Experience

Consider getting real ML work experience mid-way:

- Internal team transfer at current company
- Part-time ML consulting/freelance
- Open source maintainership
- Research collaboration with academics

This validates direction + builds resume simultaneously.
