# Performance Review - Self Assessment Questions (FY26)

## Question 1: Self Review Assessment

The self review assessment is your opportunity to reflect on your impact in FY26 - what you delivered, how you delivered it, and where you grew.

When completing your self-review, consider highlighting the following:

- Progress toward goals and key deliverables, including any commitments set at the beginning of the cycle
- The measurable business impact of your work (e.g., revenue influence, product delivery, customer outcomes, cost savings, efficiency gains, risk reduction, etc.)
- How your actions supported team, functional, and company GKSOs
- How you demonstrated Kong's COURAGE values
- Examples of where you went above expectations or influenced cross-functional success
- Challenges you faced and how you navigated them
- Areas where additional support, clarity, or development could increase your future impact

Your self-assessment helps create a complete and fair view of your performance, strengthens calibration quality, and sets the foundation for a meaningful, forward-looking conversation with your manager.

## Question 3: Self-Assessed Overall Performance Rating

In this section, you'll select a self-assessed overall performance rating that best reflects your contributions this year and explain how your results, behaviors, and impact align with that rating.

1. Select the overall rating that best describes your performance
2. Use the comment box to explain why you chose the self-assessed rating

**Rating Options:**

### Core Performer

A Core Performer reliably delivers on their commitments and meets the expectations of their role, contributing to improved business performance. Their work demonstrates meaningful impact relative to effort, helping move Kong forward in measurable ways.

Core performers drive results by solving problems and applying COURAGE values in how they get things done, not just what they do. They continue to deepen their business impact through ongoing learning, cross-functional collaboration, and continuous improvement.

### Top Performer

A Top Performer exceeds role expectations by delivering above their expected goals and deliverable that grows the business. They consistently surpass goals and drive impact that goes beyond the expected by accelerating progress, efficiency, or growth in ways that are felt across the business.

Top Performers are champions of Kong's COURAGE values, excelling not just in what they achieve, but in how they achieve it. They are seen as high-impact contributors, often operating at a strategic level, and are committed to Kong's long-term success. Whether through leadership, innovation, or influence, they raise the bar for performance.

### Exceptional Performer

An Exceptional Performer is a game changer whose contributions transform the business, redefine what's possible, and create lasting enterprise value. They not only exceed goals, but also deliver breakthrough results beyond their formal commitments, whether by building new revenue streams, saving the company millions, or introducing innovations that reshape how Kong operates internally or externally. Their work creates outsized impact, often with cross-functional visibility and recognition. They are bold in their approach, deeply aligned with COURAGE values, and bring others along in driving exceptional, enduring change to Kong.
