- **DeepSeek mHC: Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections** — New training architecture reduces compute needs while maintaining stability. Uses Sinkhorn-Knopp algorithm to project residual matrices onto Birkhoff polytope, making models easier to train without expensive restarts. Tested on 3B-27B parameter models with superior benchmark performance.
