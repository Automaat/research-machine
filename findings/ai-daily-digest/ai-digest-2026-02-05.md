## 📊 Top 3 Stories

1. **GitHub Opens Multi-Agent Frontier** — Claude & Codex join Copilot as native agents, signaling industry shift from single-assistant to multi-agent orchestration. Developers can now assign same issue to 3 different AI agents and compare approaches. Future includes Google, Cognition, xAI. This fundamentally changes how code gets written—from "AI helps me" to "I orchestrate AI teams."

2. **ElevenLabs Triples to $11B** — Voice AI valuation jump from $3.3B to $11B in 8 months validates enterprise demand for voice agents. $330M ARR shows voice AI moving from novelty to business-critical infrastructure. GDPR/HIPAA compliance through on-device processing. $0.003/min pricing + open weights (Apache 2.0) challenges cloud-only voice APIs. Privacy-first AI becoming competitive advantage in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, defense).
