## Talk Angle Suggestions

For "Agentic AI for SWE/SRE/Infra":

- **SREcon angle:** "AI agents for on-call: from alert to remediation without human in the loop" — reliability, runbooks, incident response automation
- **DevOps angle:** "Agentic CI/CD: autonomous pipelines that self-heal and optimize" — MCP, tool-calling agents in build systems
- **Platform Eng angle:** "Internal developer platforms with embedded AI agents" — reducing cognitive load, self-service infra
- **Polish conference angle:** Polish + English bilingual potential; practical case studies resonate more than theory
