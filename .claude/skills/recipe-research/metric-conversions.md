## 🧮 Quick Mental Math

- **Cups to grams:** Multiply by ~120 for flour, ~200 for sugar
- **Fahrenheit to Celsius:** Subtract 30, divide by 2 (rough estimate)
- **Ounces to grams:** Multiply by 28
- **Tablespoons to ml:** Multiply by 15

---

## ⚠️ Common Conversion Mistakes

1. **Not accounting for density** — 1 cup flour ≠ 1 cup sugar in weight
2. **Scooping vs spooning flour** — Scooped flour can be 150g vs 120g spooned
3. **Packed vs loose brown sugar** — Always pack unless specified
4. **Liquid vs dry measuring cups** — Use appropriate type
5. **Room temp butter vs cold** — Affects volume measurements
