# Recipe Verification Checklist

Use this checklist to verify recipe accuracy and food safety.

---

## 🌡️ Temperature Safety

### Internal Temperatures (Minimum Safe)

| Protein | Celsius | Fahrenheit | Notes |
|---------|---------|------------|-------|
| Chicken (whole) | 74°C | 165°F | Thigh meat |
| Chicken (breast) | 74°C | 165°F | — |
| Ground poultry | 74°C | 165°F | — |
| Turkey | 74°C | 165°F | Thigh meat |
| Duck | 74°C | 165°F | Or 57°C for medium-rare breast |
| Ground beef | 71°C | 160°F | — |
| Beef (whole cuts) | 63°C | 145°F | Medium; + 3 min rest |
| Pork | 63°C | 145°F | + 3 min rest |
| Lamb | 63°C | 145°F | Medium |
| Fish | 63°C | 145°F | Or until flakes |
| Shellfish | 63°C | 145°F | Until opaque |
| Eggs | 71°C | 160°F | For dishes with eggs |

### Beef Doneness (Steaks)

| Doneness | Internal Temp | Pull Temp (carryover) |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| Rare | 52°C (125°F) | 49°C (120°F) |
| Medium-rare | 57°C (135°F) | 54°C (130°F) |
| Medium | 63°C (145°F) | 60°C (140°F) |
| Medium-well | 68°C (155°F) | 65°C (150°F) |
| Well done | 74°C (165°F) | 71°C (160°F) |

### Danger Zone

- **4°C to 60°C (40°F to 140°F)** — bacteria multiply rapidly
- **2-hour rule** — discard food left in danger zone >2 hours
- **1-hour rule** — if ambient temp >32°C (90°F)

|------|-------|-------|

## 🚩 Red Flags to Check

### Missing Steps

- [ ] Resting time for meat after cooking (5-10 min minimum)
- [ ] Resting time for bread dough (gluten development)
- [ ] Preheating oven (especially for baking)
- [ ] Bloom gelatin in cold water first
- [ ] Toast spices/nuts before adding
- [ ] Pat proteins dry before searing
- [ ] Bring eggs/dairy to room temp for baking

### Unrealistic Times

| Red Flag | Reality |
|----------|---------|
| "Caramelize onions in 5 min" | Takes 30-45 min low heat |
| "Reduce sauce in 2 min" | Usually 10-20 min |
| "Rise dough in 15 min" | Standard rise 1-2 hours |
| "Braise meat for 30 min" | Usually 2-3+ hours |
| "Brown butter in 1 min" | Takes 3-5 min |

### Technique Sequence Issues

- [ ] Mise en place mentioned for complex recipes
- [ ] Proteins salted ahead of time (if needed)
- [ ] Acid added at correct time (can toughen proteins if too early)
- [ ] Garlic added after onions (burns faster)
- [ ] Delicate herbs added at end (basil, parsley)
- [ ] Deglazing before fond burns
- [ ] Fat hot enough before adding protein

### Ratio Red Flags

- [ ] Too much baking soda (metallic taste) — max 1/4 tsp per cup flour
- [ ] Too much salt in baking — 1/2 tsp per cup flour typical
- [ ] Insufficient liquid for braise — protein should be 1/2 to 2/3 submerged
- [ ] Too little fat for roux — 1:1 ratio required

## 🔬 Food Science Verification

### Maillard Reaction

- Requires: dry surface, high heat (>150°C/300°F), proteins + sugars
- Red flag: "Brown meat in liquid" — won't happen

### Caramelization

- Sugar melts at 160°C (320°F)
- Caramelizes at 170°C (338°F)
- Burns above 180°C (356°F)

### Emulsification

- Requires: emulsifier (egg yolk, mustard), gradual oil addition
- Red flag: Adding oil too fast — will break

### Gluten Development

- Needs: hydration, kneading or time
- Salt tightens gluten — add after initial mix for artisan bread
- Fat inhibits gluten — add after development for enriched doughs

### Protein Coagulation

- Eggs coagulate: 65-70°C (149-158°F)
- Meat proteins: 50-70°C (122-158°F)
- Collagen → gelatin: >70°C (158°F) over time

## ✅ Verification Output Format

When verifying a recipe, report:

```markdown
## Verification Results

### ✅ Passed
- Internal temperatures correct
- Ratios within normal range
- Technique sequence logical

### ⚠️ Concerns
- [CONCERN] Onion caramelization time too short (5 min stated, needs 30-45 min)
- [CONCERN] Missing resting step after removing from oven

### 🔧 Recommended Fixes
1. Increase onion cooking time to 30-45 minutes over medium-low heat
2. Add "Rest 10 minutes before slicing" after step X

### 📚 Verification Sources
- [Serious Eats - Food Science](URL)
- [USDA Safe Cooking Temperatures](URL)
```
