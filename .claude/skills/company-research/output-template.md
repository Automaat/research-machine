## Usage Notes

### Filling the Template

1. **Verdict stars** — Based on overall fit, not just company quality
2. **Risk assessment** — Be thorough, protect against bad surprises
3. **Interview prep** — Make it actionable, specific to this company
4. **Recommendation** — Clear next action

### Saving

Save completed research to:

```text
findings/job-search/research/[company-slug].md
```text

Example: `findings/job-search/research/anthropic.md`

### Updating

If new information found later, update existing file and note:

```markdown
---
*Updated YYYY-MM-DD: [What changed]*
```text
