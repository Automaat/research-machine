**KW status:**

- ✅ Clean KW: LOW risk
- ⚠️ Mortgages in Dział IV: HIGH risk [KB: finding-building-plot-poland-guide.md:121]

**Zoning status:**

- ✅ MPZP MN confirmed: LOW risk

- ❌ Major restrictions: HIGH risk
